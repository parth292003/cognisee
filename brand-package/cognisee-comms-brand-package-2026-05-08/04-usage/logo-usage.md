# Cognisee Logo Usage

## Preferred Mark

Use the horizontal symbol plus wordmark for most communications:

- `01-logos/current/cognisee-logo-horizontal-black-transparent-3000w.png`
- `01-logos/current/cognisee-logo-horizontal-cream-transparent-3000w.png`
- `01-logos/current/cognisee-logo-horizontal-white-transparent-3000w.png`

Use the symbol alone only where space is constrained, such as avatars, favicons, social profile images, or watermarking:

- `01-logos/current/cognisee-symbol-black-transparent-1600.png`
- `01-logos/current/cognisee-symbol-cream-transparent-1600.png`
- `01-logos/current/cognisee-symbol-white-transparent-1600.png`

## Backgrounds

- On dark green-black backgrounds, use the cream or white transparent logo.
- On milky cream/light backgrounds, use the black transparent logo.
- Do not place the black logo on dark backgrounds.
- Do not place the cream/white logo on light backgrounds unless contrast is very high.

## Clearspace

Keep clearspace around the logo roughly equal to the height of one symbol node/dot. Avoid placing it against edges, dense photography, or heavy texture.

## Do Not

- Do not stretch, skew, outline, glow, bevel, or recolor the logo outside the palette.
- Do not add drop shadows.
- Do not place the logo inside a card unless the composition requires it.
- Do not mix the current horizontal mark with the older triangle/pyramid logo in external materials.

## Current Website Treatment

The website uses the black transparent horizontal PNG and applies this filter in dark mode:

```css
filter: invert(1) brightness(1.08);
```

For comms work, prefer using the prepared cream or white PNG directly instead of relying on CSS filters.
