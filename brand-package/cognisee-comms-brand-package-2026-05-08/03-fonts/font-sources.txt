Cognisee font stack, May 2026

Display:
Newsreader
Use for H1, H2, H3, and major editorial headings.

Body:

https://fonts.google.com/specimen/IBM+Plex+Sans

https://github.com/IBM/plex

Use for nav, eyebrow, metadata, small labels, and technical links.
Google Fonts specimen:
https://fonts.google.com/specimen/IBM+Plex+Mono
Source repository:
https://github.com/IBM/plex
